## 1. Overview

Navius GPS is an open-source navigation app for Ubuntu Touch. It can be used **without an account and without connecting to Navius servers**, in which case no personal data is collected or transmitted, including vehicle position.

This policy applies only when the user chooses to use the **online services operated by Navius**:

- **Valhalla** (`valhalla.egpsistemas.com`) — route calculation and predictive traffic
- **Marlin** (`navius-maps.egpsistemas.com`) — vector map tiles
- **Photon** (`navius-maps.egpsistemas.com/photon`) — address and point-of-interest search
- **Overpass Navius** (`navius-maps.egpsistemas.com/overpass`) — speed cameras and OSM data
- **Navius API** (`navius-api.egpsistemas.com`) — community alerts, road ads, trip sharing, system messages, settings sync

## 3. Data collected and purposes

| Data | Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| GPS position during navigation | Calculate routes and predictive traffic (Valhalla server) | Consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR) |
| Alert location | Show nearby community alerts (accidents, roadworks, speed cameras) | Consent |
| GPS position | Show geolocated ads along the route | Consent |
| GPS position in real time | Share trip live (optional feature, explicitly initiated by user) | Consent |
| Anonymous device identifier | Receive system messages and manage the session | Legitimate interest (service operation) |
| Email and password (hashed) | Account authentication | Consent / contract performance |

**Not collected:** real name, phone number, contacts, past route history (except what is saved locally by the user). Data is never sold to third parties.

Apart from sharing location when commercial ads must be displayed on the map in order to place the ad nearby, and for Valhalla traffic training, data will not be used for any commercial purpose. By accepting the data collection described above, the user does not consent to its redistribution to third parties, sale, or any other use thereof.

If in the future it were necessary to share data for commercial purposes, the user will be notified of the changes, and no data will be collected without explicit consent.

This data is collected solely to enable functionality that would not otherwise be possible. The application's policy is never based on the exploitation of personal data.

## 4. Navius-operated servers

Services that send location data to Navius servers:

- **Navius Valhalla** (`valhalla.egpsistemas.com`): routing and predictive traffic.
- **Marlin** (`navius-maps.egpsistemas.com`): vector map tiles and photo server.
- **Photon** (`navius-maps.egpsistemas.com/photon`): address and POI search.
- **Navius Overpass** (`navius-maps.egpsistemas.com/overpass`): speed cameras.
- **Navius API** (`navius-api.egpsistemas.com`): community alerts, ads, trip sharing, system messages.

---

## 5. Using Navius without sharing data

You can use Navius **without sending any data to our servers** by configuring:

- **Routing:** OSM Scout Server (offline) or another public Valhalla server.
- **Maps:** cached tiles or your own tile server.
- **Search:** public Photon or another geocoding server.

In that case, no account or acceptance of this policy is required.

---

## 6. Data retention

Location data transmitted for routing and alerts is **not stored** on our servers beyond the time needed to process the request (seconds). Account data (email) is kept while the account is active. Aggregated telemetry may be kept for up to 90 days for service improvement purposes.
